How do Google's service-area rules work, and what if I list an address?

Short answer

Google's help page says a business that does not serve customers at its address should remove the address and list a service area. It allows up to 20 service areas, within about two hours of driving from the business base, and no radius.

Checked against Google's pages, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

Service-area or hybrid

Service-area business: A business that visits or delivers to customers directly but doesn't serve customers at their business address.

Hybrid business: A business that serves customers at its business address but also directly visits or delivers to them.

From Manage your service areas for service-area & hybrid businesses, Google Business Profile Help.

Limits on the service area

You can have up to 20 service areas.

The boundaries of your overall area shouldn't be more than about 2 hours of driving time from where your business is based.

You can't set your service area as a radius distance around your business.

Service-area businesses can only have one profile for the whole area that they serve.

From Manage your service areas for service-area & hybrid businesses, Google Business Profile Help.

Other details Google lists

Businesses with products or services that have an age requirement like alcohol, cannabis, or weapons, need to have a storefront.

If approved, service area edits may take up to 48 hours to show on your Business Profile.
