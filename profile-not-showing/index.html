My Google Business Profile doesn't show up when I search for my business. Why?

Google says to show your business on Search and Maps you must add or claim it and then verify it

Full short answer

Google says to show your business on Search and Maps you must add or claim it and then verify it, and verification reviews take up to 5 business days. A profile that counts as a duplicate won't show on Search or Maps. Edits usually take about 10 minutes and sometimes up to 30 days.
