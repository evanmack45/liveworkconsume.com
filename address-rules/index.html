Which addresses does Google allow on a Business Profile? Virtual offices, shared offices, and homes

Short answer

Google's help pages say a virtual office is not eligible, and a co-working office needs signage, customer contact, and staff during business hours. A P.O. box or remote mailbox is not acceptable. A business run from a home can hide its address and use a service area.

Checked against Google's pages on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we do not file appeals.

Virtual offices and mailboxes

If your business rents a physical mailing address but doesn't operate out of that location, also known as a virtual office, that location isn't eligible for a Business Profile.

P.O. boxes or mailboxes located at remote locations aren't acceptable.

Enter your physical address as "Address Line 1." Enter your mailbox or suite number as "Address Line 2."

From Guidelines for representing your business on Google, Google Business Profile Help.

Co-working spaces

Businesses can't list an office at a co-working space unless that office maintains clear signage, receives customers at the location during business hours, and is staffed during business hours by your business staff.

From Guidelines for representing your business on Google, Google Business Profile Help.

Signage and customer contact

Businesses showing their address on Google should maintain permanent fixed signage of their business name at the address.

From Guidelines for representing your business on Google, Google Business Profile Help.

To qualify for a Business Profile, a business must make in-person contact with customers during its stated hours.

From Business eligibility and ownership guidelines, Google Business Profile Help.

Home-based businesses

If you're a service-area business, you should hide your business address from customers. For example, if you're a plumber and run your business from your residential address, clear the address from your Business Profile.

From Guidelines for representing your business on Google, Google Business Profile Help.

The page Service-area business rules covers the limits that come with hiding the address.

What these pages do not say

  • Whether two unrelated businesses can share one address. The pages give rules for co-working offices and for departments, not for this case.
  • Whether Google will accept a specific address. It decides from your business information and other sources.
