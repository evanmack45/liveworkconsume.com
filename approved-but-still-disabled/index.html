Google approved my appeal, but my Business Profile is still disabled or not visible. Why?

Short answer

Google's help pages do not explain a delay after an approved appeal. They do point to three checks: which decision the appeal covered, whether an account restriction came first, and whether the tool status changed for the right profile. For problems after reinstatement, Google points to Business Profile Help.

Checked against Google's pages on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we do not file appeals.

Check which decision you appealed

The appeals tool asks you to select the decision you want to appeal. The article says the tool shows the date of the initial decision, the reason for the decision, a link to the violated policy, whether the decision can be appealed, and the status of the decision.

The status field uses these labels: Submitted, Approved, Not Approved, Can't be appealed, and Eligible for appeal. Open the tool and read the status for the exact decision you appealed.

An account appeal is not a profile appeal

If your approved appeal was for the Google Account, the profile suspension is a separate step. Google's article says:

Once the account restriction is lifted, to remove the Business Profile suspension, you can submit an appeal for your Business Profile.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

Google's policy overview adds:

The account must be reinstated before any previously removed content can be successfully reinstated or new content added to the profile.

From Overview of Google Business Profile policies, Google Business Profile Help.

The page Google Account restricted covers the order of those two appeals.

If you appealed many profiles at once

For more than 10 profiles, Google says the tool tracks one profile:

Note: In the Google Business Profiles appeals tool, the status will only change for the profile you selected.

You will only receive one email notification with an appeal decision for the profile you selected in the tool.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

What Google says to do after reinstatement

If you have issues with your reinstated profile, contact Google Business Profile Help.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

Google's help articles do not describe missing reviews or photos after a reinstatement. One line on the subject appears in the Google Business Profile Community, in a thread where a business owner pasted an email they said came from Google support. We cannot confirm the email. The owner's copy reads:

If your reviews went missing after we reinstated your disabled or suspended profile, reply to this email. We'll investigate for you.

From Reviews all gone after Google reinstated business, a user-posted thread in the Google Business Profile Community.

That is a user's copy of a support email, not a help article. Treat it as a lead, not as a policy.

What these pages do not say

  • How long a profile takes to show again after an approval. The pages quoted here give no time. The one figure Google gives for appeals is on how long reinstatement takes.
  • Why a profile can stay hidden after an approved appeal.

We cannot see your profile and we cannot tell you why Google made its decision. The appeals tool shows the reason for each decision.
