Google approved my appeal, but my Business Profile is still disabled or not visible. Why?

Short answer

Google's help pages do not explain a delay after an approved appeal.

Full short answer

Google's help pages do not explain a delay after an approved appeal. They do point to three checks: which decision the appeal covered, whether an account restriction came first, and whether the tool status changed for the right profile. For problems after reinstatement, Google points to Business Profile Help.

Checked against Google's pages, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

Check which decision you appealed

The appeals tool asks you to select the decision you want to appeal. The article says the tool shows the date of the initial decision, the reason for the decision, a link to the violated policy, whether the decision can be appealed, and the status of the decision.

The status field uses these labels: Submitted, Approved, Not Approved, Can't be appealed, and Eligible for appeal. Open the tool and read the status for the exact decision you appealed.

An account appeal is not a profile appeal

If your approved appeal was for the Google Account, the profile suspension is a separate step. Google's article says:
