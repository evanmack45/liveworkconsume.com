Google says my Business Profile was suspended for deceptive content. What do its rules say?

Short answer

The Business Profile policy pages we read never use the words deceptive content. The closest rules cover your business name, a change of business identity, and a business that Google decides does not exist at its address. This page quotes them and does not diagnose your profile.

Checked against Google's pages, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

What the pages say about names and identity

Read it in: Guidelines for representing your business on Google.

Merchants must accurately represent their business identity.

From Overview of Google Business Profile policies, Google Business Profile Help.

A business that Google decides does not exist

Read it in: Overview of Google Business Profile policies.

The word misrepresentation

Google's policy overview links its identity rule to a misrepresentation policy. That policy is part of the Maps content policy, which is written about content people post on Google Maps. It says:

we don't allow individuals to use Google Maps to mislead or deceive others, or make misrepresentations

From Prohibited & restricted content, Maps User Generated Content Policy, Google Maps policy help.

These pages do not say which policy applied to your profile. The appeals tool lists the reason for each decision and a link to the policy Google says was violated.
