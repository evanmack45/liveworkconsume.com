I'm changing my business name. Can I keep my Google Business Profile?

Short answer

Google says a minor name change, where the proper nouns and services in the name and the category stay the same, can be made on the existing profile.

Full short answer

Google says a minor name change, where the proper nouns and services in the name and the category stay the same, can be made on the existing profile. A name change that does not meet that test is a new business: you mark the old profile closed and create a new one.
