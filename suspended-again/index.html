Google suspended my Business Profile again after reinstating it. Which rules apply?

Short answer

Google's help pages do not explain why a reinstated profile is suspended again. They list rules that can cause a suspension, and they say a pattern of violations can restrict the account. Read the reason in the appeals tool first, then compare your profile with the rules below.

Checked against Google's pages on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we do not file appeals.

Read the reason Google shows you

The appeals tool asks you to select the decision you want to appeal. It shows the reason for that decision and a link to the policy Google says was violated. Start there, because these pages cannot see your profile.

Rules Google names that can lead to a suspension

Business name:

Including unnecessary information in your business name isn't permitted, and could result in the suspension of your Business Profile.

From Guidelines for representing your business on Google, Google Business Profile Help.

Mailbox address:

Merchants that set their business address at a P.O. Box will have their profile suspended.

From Overview of Google Business Profile policies, Google Business Profile Help.

A change of business identity:

Edits that attempt to significantly change the nature of a business are often fraudulent or indicate that the business listing should be removed.

From Overview of Google Business Profile policies, Google Business Profile Help.

More than one profile for one location:

Do not create more than one page for each location of your business, either in a single account or multiple accounts.

From Guidelines for representing your business on Google, Google Business Profile Help.

A new profile for the same business:

Important: Do not create a new Business Profile for the same business.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

A pattern of violations

If a merchant displays a pattern of violating Google Business Profile policies, they may have their Business Profile access restricted.

If a merchant's account is restricted, all Business Profiles associated with that account will be suspended.

From Overview of Google Business Profile policies, Google Business Profile Help.

The page Google Account restricted covers what Google says to do next.

Fixing an address after reinstatement

If your business has not moved and you need to fix an incorrect address, you must contact us.

From Overview of Google Business Profile policies, Google Business Profile Help.

What these pages do not say

  • Why your profile was suspended a second time.
  • How many suspensions lead to an account restriction. The pages say "a pattern" and give no number.
  • Whether an edit you made after reinstatement caused the new suspension.

We cannot see your profile and we cannot tell you why Google made its decision. The appeals tool shows the reason for each decision.
