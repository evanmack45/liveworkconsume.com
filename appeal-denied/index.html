Your Google Business Profile appeal was denied. What do Google's help pages say next?

Short answer

Google's help page says an appeal can be submitted once.

Full short answer

Google's help page says an appeal can be submitted once. If the appeal is denied, Google says it may be able to do an additional review, and you request it with a separate form. The pages do not say how long a review takes or what decides it.

Checked against Google's pages, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

The additional review is a separate form, not a second pass through the appeals tool.

What the additional review form asks for

The form is titled Request additional review of a denied appeal. It opens with a condition, then asks for three details: the date the profile was suspended or disabled, the official email address used to manage the profile, and the Business Profile ID.

It also asks you to attach two documents. Google's wording:
