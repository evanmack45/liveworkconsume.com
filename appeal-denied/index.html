Your Google Business Profile appeal was denied. What do Google's help pages say next?

Short answer

Google's help page says an appeal can be submitted once. If the appeal is denied, Google says it may be able to do an additional review, and you request it with a separate form. The pages do not say how long a review takes or what decides it.

Checked against Google's pages on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we do not file appeals.

What Google says about a denied appeal

The article Fix suspended or disabled profiles has a section titled Request an additional review to a denied request. It says:

Only if your reinstatement request is denied, we may be able to do an additional review to prove your eligibility.

You can provide additional evidence that wasn't added with your original appeal.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

In the steps for a first appeal, the same article says:

Note: Appeals can only be submitted once.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

The additional review is a separate form, not a second pass through the appeals tool.

What the additional review form asks for

The form is titled Request additional review of a denied appeal. It opens with a condition:

You will not be able to submit the request for additional review until you appeal using the Google Business Profile Appeals tool and your appeal is denied.

From Request additional review of a denied appeal, Google Business Profile Help form.

It asks for three details: the date the profile was suspended or disabled, the official email address used to manage the profile, and the Business Profile ID.

It also asks you to attach two documents. Google's wording:

Your Business Registration / License displaying the business name and address that matches the listing you are appealing for.

A utility bill displaying the same business name and address as your registration / license, or tax certificates.

Avoid sending sensitive business documents.

From Request additional review of a denied appeal, Google Business Profile Help form.

After you submit

The form's confirmation text says:

Reinstatement request received. Please note, you will not receive an email confirmation.

Our team will next contact you as soon as we have reviewed your appeal for reinstatement.

From Request additional review of a denied appeal, Google Business Profile Help form.

For readers in the European Economic Area, the first article adds:

If you're located in a European Economic Area (EEA) member state or territory, you may have additional redress options.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

What these pages do not say

  • How long an additional review takes. Neither page gives a time.
  • What makes Google approve or deny. The pages name types of evidence but give no decision rule.
  • What to do if the additional review is also denied.

We cannot see your profile and we cannot tell you why Google made its decision. The appeals tool shows the reason for each decision.
