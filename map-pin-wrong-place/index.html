My Google Maps pin for my business is in the wrong place. How do I fix it?

Short answer

In your Business Profile, open Edit profile, then Location, and choose Adjust to move the pin. Google says edits usually show in about 10 minutes and in some cases take up to 30 days. Its help page covers your own profile's pin, Street View and wrong directions.

What Google says about the pin

Google's page on your business address says:

You can also pin your location on the map to show where your business is.

From Manage your business address, Google Business Profile Help.

The steps: go to your Business Profile, select Edit profile, then Location. Next to Business location, select Edit, then Adjust. Move the map so the pin points to your business location, then select Done and Save.
