Will I lose my reviews if my Google Business Profile is suspended?

Short answer

Google's pages do not say a suspension deletes your reviews. They say reviews removed for policy violations will not be restored, and that reviews sometimes go missing after reinstatement. Google's tip is to contact support if that happens. Google also says not to create a new profile, which would start with none.

Checked against Google's pages on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we do not file appeals.

What Google says about missing reviews

Google's page on missing or delayed reviews gives several reasons. Two of them:

Reviews are checked to ensure they comply with our policies. In some cases, this process might take a few days, which can delay a review's appearance on your profile.

Reviews removed for policy violations won't be restored.

From About missing or delayed reviews, Google Business Profile Help.

It then has a tip about reinstatement:

Tip: Sometimes reviews might be removed after a Business Profile is reinstated. If this happens, contact support for assistance.

From About missing or delayed reviews, Google Business Profile Help.

While the profile is suspended

Google's article on suspended profiles says what a removal means for the public and for you:

The public can't go to the profile.

The owner and managers can't act on the profile.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

For a reinstated profile with a problem:

If you have issues with your reinstated profile, contact Google Business Profile Help.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

What a Product Expert said in the Community

In a thread from October 2026 an owner reports that reviews were missing after reinstatement. A reply in the thread says:

It is common to not have the reviews visible right after a reinstatement. Usually within a few days they will reappear.

From Profile Reactivated After Suspension, But All Reviews Are Missing, a user-posted thread in the Google Business Profile Community.

That reply is from a user of the Community forum, not a Google help article. Treat it as a report, not a policy. The page Appeal approved but the profile is still disabled has a second thread on the same problem.

Do not start over with a new profile

Important: Do not create a new Business Profile for the same business.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

See Can I create a new profile after a suspension.

What these pages do not say

  • That a suspension deletes reviews. The pages quoted here do not say it, and they do not say it never happens.
  • How long reviews take to come back. Google's pages give no time. The Community reply says "a few days" and is not Google policy.
  • Whether your reviews will return. Only Google can see your profile.
