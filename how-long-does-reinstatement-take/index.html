How long does a Google Business Profile reinstatement take after an appeal?

Google gives one number.

Full short answer

Google gives one number. Appeal reviews and decisions can take up to 5 business days. Google gives no total from suspension to reinstatement, and no time for an account appeal, an additional review, or a new verification. Forum posts report longer waits, but those are reports, not Google policy.

The one number Google gives

Google's page on appeals says:

Read it in: Appeal Business Profile content & profile restrictions.

The appeal steps end with an email:

Google will review your appeal and send you an email with the appeal decision.

From Appeal Business Profile content & profile restrictions, Google Business Profile Help.

What that number does not cover

If an account restriction came first, there are two appeals, one after the other. Google's article says:

Read it in: Fix suspended or disabled profiles.

If an appeal is denied, there is a further step:

Read it in: Request additional review of a denied appeal.

Google's pages give no time for the account appeal or for the additional review. If Google asks you to verify, that is its own review:

Read it in: Verify your business on Google.

So the 5 business days is a figure for one review. A case with several reviews in a row has several waits.
