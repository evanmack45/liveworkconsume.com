My Google Business Profile photos were rejected or are stuck pending. What does Google say?

Short answer

Google lists the standards: JPG or PNG, 10 KB to 5 MB, at least 250 by 250 pixels, in focus and not heavily altered.

Full short answer

Google lists the standards: JPG or PNG, 10 KB to 5 MB, at least 250 by 250 pixels, in focus and not heavily altered. A photo shows as Pending while it is processing or if the profile is not verified, and Not approved if it was flagged for breaking the policies. Neither status shows to customers.
