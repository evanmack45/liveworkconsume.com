I have a duplicate Google Business Profile. How do I merge it or remove it?

Short answer

Google gives different routes. If you created the duplicate, you can remove it without affecting your verified listing, but removal is permanent for that profile's content and managers. To merge two profiles that exist on Maps, report a suggestion on Google Maps. A merge combines the reviews, and replies to reviews can be lost.

Checked against Google's pages, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

Why a profile is called a duplicate

Google gives three reasons a profile is treated as a duplicate: a verified profile already exists for the same business, several profiles share an address, or several profiles for one business offer different services. If the verified profile belongs to someone else, see Someone else owns my profile.
