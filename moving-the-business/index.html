We're moving. Do we update our Google Business Profile or make a new one?

Google says that if your business relocates, you should not create a new Business Profile for the new location.

Full short answer

Google says that if your business relocates, you should not create a new Business Profile for the new location. Update the existing profile with the new address. Google's edit page also says that if you move to a new address after it's verified, you must verify your business again.
