Tip: If you look for your Business Profile and can’t find the "Claim this business" or "Own this business" button, you might already be the owner. Learn how to find your profile . Troubleshooting Business Profile Ownership Request ownership of a Business Profile To request ownership of a storefront or hybrid business from the current Business Profile owner: Go to business.google.com/add . Enter the name and address of your business. Choose your business from the list. You'll get a message that says “Someone else may manage this Business Profile.” Select Request Access . If you're authorized to manage the business, you can request ownership from the current profile owner. Fill out the form. Select Submit . Tips: To request ownership of a service-area business that provides services at the customer's location and doesn’t have a physical shop or office, contact us . In the "Tell us what we can help with" field, enter Transfer ownership of listing . When you submit your request, select Transfer ownership of listing as the issue description. If you have a bulk-verified account, you can also use the bulk upload spreadsheet method to request ownership of existing Business Profiles. Learn how to verify Business Profiles in bulk . What happens after you request ownership After you request ownership, you’ll get an email confirmation. The current profile owner is then notified by email and has 3 days to respond. To check the status of your request, sign in to your Business Profile or select the link in your confirmation email. If your request is: Approved: You'll get an approval email and can now manage the Business Profile. Learn how to manage your business information Learn about limits for new profile owners & managers Denied: You'll get a rejection email but you can still suggest an edit to the profile . You can also try to appeal the decision. If you don’t get a response after 3 days, you may have the option to claim the profile. To claim a Business Profile: Open the confirmation email you got about your ownership request. Select View request Verify . Follow the on-screen instructions to verify your business. You can sign in to your Business Profile and look for a “Claim” or “Verify” button. Tip: The option to claim a profile isn’t always available.
