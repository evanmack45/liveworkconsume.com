This help content & information General Help Center experience Close Next Help Center Community Get-Started Guide Google Business Profile Manage your business information Manage your business hours Edit your business hours Edit your business hours You can set and edit your business’s hours on your Business Profile for Google Maps and Search. This helps customers to find your business’s most up to date and accurate hours of operation for a normal week. Learn more about business hours guidelines . Manage your business Set main business hours Go to your Business Profile . Select Edit profile Hours . In the "Business hours" section, next to "Hours," select Edit Open with main hours . If you don’t want your business hours to show on your profile, click Open with no main hours Save . Next to each day of the week that your business is open, check the box. To set hours, use the dropdowns. After you set your hours, click Save . Set business hour breaks To indicate your business hour breaks, create 2 entries for the same day with different times. Go to your Business Profile . Select Edit profile Hours . In the "Business hours" section, next to "Hours," select Edit Open with main hours . Next to the day of the week that your business is open, check the box. Example: If your business is open from 9 AM–2 PM on Saturdays and closed from 2 PM–5 PM, then open again from 5 PM–10 PM: Check the box next to “Saturday.” To set the start of your business hour break: Click Opens at 9:00 AM . Click Closes at 2:00 PM . Click Add hours . To set the end of your business hour break: Click Opens at 5:00 PM . Click Closes at 10:00 PM . Click Save . Set 24 hour business hours Each day of the week that your business is open, click Opens at 24 hours Save . Set Special hours & More hours You can let your customers know about changes in your business hours or when certain services are available. For days when you have unusual hours, such as holidays or special events, you can set Special hours . Special hours are when your business: Temporarily adjusts its hours of operation. Remains closed for 6 days in a row or fewer. If you offer specific services with different times such as senior hours, or delivery and takeout hours, you can set More hours . How to generate hours Important: This feature is currently available for select regions and languages and may not be available for all users. You can use this feature to more easily update your business hours using natural language input. For example, "open Mon-Fri, 9AM-5PM but closed for lunch 1PM-2PM. Serving breakfast everyday 9-11AM." Go to your Business Profile . Select Edit profile Hours . In the text box in the "Generate hours" section, describe your hours of operation. You can be specific about days and times, including breaks or 24-hour operation. Select Generate hours . Based on the tool's understanding of your described hours, your business hours are updated with AI. If needed, you can manually update your hours. To confirm the changes, select Save . Edit posts on Business Profile about your updated hours After you update your hours, customers can find: On your Business Profile: A message that confirms your recent update. On the Updates tab of your profile in mobile devices: A post that confirms you made a change to your hours. To give more info about your updated hours, edit your post. To remove the hours update message and the post, delete your associated post. Learn how to edit or delete a post on Google .
