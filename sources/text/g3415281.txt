This help content & information General Help Center experience Close Next Help Center Community Get-Started Guide Google Business Profile Manage profile ownership Transfer primary ownership of a Business Profile Transfer primary ownership of a Business Profile In some cases, you might want to transfer primary ownership of a Business Profile to an existing owner or manager. If you’re the only user associated with your profile, you can add more owners and managers . Important: If you want to transfer ownership of your business, make sure you’re transferring primary ownership of your Business Profile to the new owner. This ensures all of your business info, like reviews, are maintained. Manage your business Understand Business Profile owner & manager roles The 2 types of roles, owners and managers, for Business Profiles have different levels of access to the profile : Only owners can add or remove users. Managers can remove themselves from a profile. You can’t add Google Groups as managers or owners. Only the primary owner can transfer primary ownership. Please note, Co Owners upgrade themselves to Primary Owners. Learn how to manage your Business Profile owners and managers . Transfer primary ownership Important: Only owners can change access roles for other owners and managers. This includes primary ownership of the Business Profile. Go to your Business Profile . Select More Business Profile settings People and access . Select the user you’d like to transfer primary ownership to. Next to “Access,” select Edit . Select Primary owner Save . Understand new owners & managers limitations The new owner or manager of a Business Profile must wait 7 days before they can manage all the features. During these 7 days, they might get an error if they: Delete or undelete a profile. Remove other owners or managers from a profile. Make themselves or someone else the primary owner. If the new owner or manager leaves the business within the first 7 days, they’ll be removed from the profile. If they change their mind, they’ll need to be added again. Tip: If an existing owner or manager tries to transfer primary ownership of the profile in charge within their first 7 days, they’ll get an error.
