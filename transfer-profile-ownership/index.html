I bought a business. How do I get its Google Business Profile transferred to me?

Short answer

Google's order is to have the current owner add you as an owner, then transfer primary ownership to you. Google says transferring primary ownership to the new owner keeps the profile's business info, such as reviews. A new owner has to wait 7 days before managing all features, and a transfer inside that window gets an error.
