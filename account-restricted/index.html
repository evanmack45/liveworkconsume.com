My Google Account is restricted. Is that the same as a suspended Business Profile?

Short answer

No. Google's help page describes an account restriction and a profile suspension as separate. A restriction suspends the profiles you manage, and you appeal the account first, on the My accounts page. After the restriction is lifted, you appeal the profile.

Checked against Google's pages on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we do not file appeals.

What an account restriction does

Your account may be restricted when you violate our policy. As a result of an account restriction, the Business Profiles you manage are suspended and you won't be able to create or claim other profiles.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

The order Google gives

To lift the restriction, you can provide a reason and submit an appeal for your account on your My accounts page.

You can find and track the status of your appeal there as well.

Once the account restriction is lifted, to remove the Business Profile suspension, you can submit an appeal for your Business Profile.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

What can lead to a restriction

If a merchant displays a pattern of violating Google Business Profile policies, they may have their Business Profile access restricted.

When a Business Profile is created via an account associated with another Google product, restriction of the non-Business Profile product account may result in suspension of the Business Profile.

The non-Business Profile account must be reinstated before reinstating the profile.

From Overview of Google Business Profile policies, Google Business Profile Help.

An account that is not in good standing

The overview article covers a Google Account that is disabled, suspended, or deleted. It says:

a merchant must take steps to fix the issue before they can access their Google Business Profile.

From Overview of Google Business Profile policies, Google Business Profile Help.

What these pages do not say

  • A second appeal for the account. The additional review section is about a denied reinstatement request, and its form asks about an appeal in the Business Profile Appeals tool.
  • Which policy violation caused your restriction. The appeal on the My accounts page is where Google lists it.

We cannot see your profile and we cannot tell you why Google made its decision. The appeals tool shows the reason for each decision.
