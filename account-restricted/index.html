My Google Account is restricted. Is that the same as a suspended Business Profile?

No.

Full short answer

No. Google's help page describes an account restriction and a profile suspension as separate. A restriction suspends the profiles you manage, and you appeal the account first, on the My accounts page. After the restriction is lifted, you appeal the profile.

What an account restriction does

Your account may be restricted when you violate our policy. As a result of an account restriction, the Business Profiles you manage are suspended and you won't be able to create or claim other profiles.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

The order Google gives

To lift the restriction, you can provide a reason and submit an appeal for your account on your My accounts page.

You can find and track the status of your appeal there as well.

Once the account restriction is lifted, to remove the Business Profile suspension, you can submit an appeal for your Business Profile.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.
