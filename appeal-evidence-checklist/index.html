What evidence does Google list for a Business Profile appeal?

What to gather

Google names four kinds of evidence: official business registration, a business license, tax certificates, and utility bills for the business.

Full short answer

Google names four kinds of evidence: official business registration, a business license, tax certificates, and utility bills for the business. The name and address on each document must match the profile. Google says an appeal can be submitted once, so gather the documents before you submit.

Checked against Google's pages, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

Documents Google names

Google's article introduces the list with this sentence:

Evidence that can help strengthen your appeal includes:

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.
  • Official business registration
  • A business license
  • Tax certificates
  • Utility bills for the business, such as electricity, phone, water, and internet

The additional review form lists more utility types:

Utilities may include any of the following: electricity, phone, gas, water, sewage, trash, recycling, TV, internet.

From Request additional review of a denied appeal, Google Business Profile Help form.

Before you submit

Avoid sending sensitive business documents.

From Request additional review of a denied appeal, Google Business Profile Help form.

A checklist from those lines

  • Write down the business name and address exactly as the profile shows them.
  • Find one official registration, license, or tax certificate that shows the same name and address.
  • Find one utility bill for the business that shows the same name and address.
  • Compare each document with the profile before you open the appeals tool.
  • Read the guidelines page on names and addresses if a document does not match.
  • Attach everything in one submission, because Google says an appeal can be submitted once.
