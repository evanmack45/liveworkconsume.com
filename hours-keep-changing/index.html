My business hours on Google keep changing back. Why, and how do I stop it?

Short answer

Google says a profile can change without you editing it.

Full short answer

Google says a profile can change without you editing it. Users suggest edits, and Google also updates profiles from sources such as user reports and licensed content. For certain suggested edits Google notifies you and you have 4 days to accept or reject.
