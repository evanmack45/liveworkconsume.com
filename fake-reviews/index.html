Someone left fake reviews on my Google Business Profile. Can I get them removed?

You can report any review, but Google says only reviews that violate its policies are eligible for removal.

Full short answer

You can report any review, but Google says only reviews that violate its policies are eligible for removal. Report from Read reviews, check the status in the Reviews Management Tool, and use the one-time appeal if the answer is no policy violation. Google says it does not get involved in conflict between businesses and customers.
