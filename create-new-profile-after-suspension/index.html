Can I create a new Google Business Profile if mine was suspended?

Short answer

Google's pages say not to. They say: "Do not create a new Business Profile for the same business." If your account is restricted, Google says you will not be able to create or claim other profiles. The route Google names is an appeal, and a reinstated profile comes back with its owner and managers.

Checked against Google's pages on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we do not file appeals.

What Google says

The article on suspended profiles says, in the appeal steps:

Important: Do not create a new Business Profile for the same business.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

The appeals article repeats it:

Do not create a new Business Profile for the same business.

From Appeal Business Profile content & profile restrictions, Google Business Profile Help.

If your account is restricted

As a result of an account restriction, the Business Profiles you manage are suspended and you won't be able to create or claim other profiles.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

The page Google Account restricted covers the order of the account appeal and the profile appeal.

What reinstatement does

Google describes what happens when it removes a profile:

The owner and managers can ask us to reinstate the profile. We reinstate both the profile and its owner and managers.

From Fix suspended or disabled profiles, Google Business Profile Help.

What these pages do not say

  • What Google does to a second profile created at the same address. The pages say not to create one and give no penalty.
  • Whether a new profile ever works. Forum users report mixed results, and we did not test it.
  • Whether you can create a profile for a different business at the same address. That is a question for the eligibility guidelines.

We cannot see your profile and we cannot tell you why Google made its decision. The appeals tool shows the reason for each decision.
