My Business Profile video verification failed. What does Google say to do next?

Short answer

Google says a failed video is marked not successful, and you can record a new one. On your phone, tap Review issues, read the examples, and include everything from the first video plus what was missing. Google also lists phone, email, mail, and live video call when available, and says to contact support if you still cannot verify.

Checked against Google's pages on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we do not file appeals.

What Google says happens after a failed video

Google's video verification page describes two results:

Not successful: Your video didn't meet all the requirements. You can record a new one for verification. We'll share the reasons why your first video wasn't accepted.

From Verify your business with a video recording, Google Business Profile Help.

It gives examples of the reasons:

Business name not visible on storefront. No nearby area shown. Missing proof of authorization to operate the business.

From Verify your business with a video recording, Google Business Profile Help.

To record again, the page says to open your Business Profile on your phone and tap Review issues:

To help you create a new video that meets all the requirements, review the issues.

All the info you showed in your first video.

All the missing info needed to meet the requirements.

From Verify your business with a video recording, Google Business Profile Help.

Rules every video must meet

An unedited, unique, and complete recording with no breaks.

At least 30 seconds long.

You must capture the footage live as pre-recorded videos aren't allowed, and you can't record it offline and upload it later.

From Verify your business with a video recording, Google Business Profile Help.

For a service-area business, Google says to show where the business operates, proof that it exists, and proof of management. It also says:

If you don't serve customers at your business location, do not add your business address to your Business Profile.

From Verify your business with a video recording, Google Business Profile Help.

Other ways to verify

Google's verify page says it picks the methods:

Verification methods are automatically determined by Google and can't be changed.

From Verify your business on Google, Google Business Profile Help.

It lists phone or text, email, live video call, and mail. On the live video call it says:

Live video verification may not be available for all businesses. We recommend that you try the options available to you. If you're still unable to verify your business, contact support.

Live video calls are only available within the business hours of your location.

From Verify your business on Google, Google Business Profile Help.

You can read your status in the verification status tool. Google describes it this way:

To manage your business information on Google Search and Maps, your listing must be verified.

From Check your verification status, Google Business Profile Help.

What these pages do not say

  • Why your video failed. Only Google can see that, and the Review issues screen shows it.
  • A form named "no other ways to verify." A Reddit reply describes one. The Google pages we read say only to contact support.
  • How many videos you can submit.

We cannot see your profile and we cannot tell you why Google made its decision. The appeals tool shows the reason for each decision.
