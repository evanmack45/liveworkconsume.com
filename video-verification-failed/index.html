My Business Profile video verification failed. What does Google say to do next?

Google says a failed video is marked not successful, and you can record a new one.

Full short answer

Google says a failed video is marked not successful, and you can record a new one. On your phone, tap Review issues, read the examples, and include everything from the first video plus what was missing. Google also lists phone, email, mail, and live video call when available, and says to contact support if you still cannot verify.

What Google says happens after a failed video

Google's video verification page describes two results, and it gives examples of the reasons.

To record again, the page says to open your Business Profile on your phone and tap Review issues.
